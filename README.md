# Washington Commanders x Amazon Stadium Naming Rights Valuation
Hypothetical naming rights valuation and partnership strategy exploring Amazon as the naming rights partner for a new Washington Commanders stadium. The project combines financial modeling, comparable stadium transactions, brand alignment, and potential sponsorship activations to recommend an annual partnership value.

## Overview
This project evaluates the potential value of an Amazon naming rights partnership with the Washington Commanders.

The proposed partnership would connect Amazon’s technology, media, and consumer services with the stadium experience. It would also give Amazon another opportunity to invest in the Washington, D.C. market while helping the Commanders improve the fan experience and strengthen the team’s brand perception.

## Objectives
- Estimate the annual value of the stadium naming rights
- Build a discounted cash flow model using attendance, media exposure, and customer acquisition assumptions
- Benchmark the valuation against comparable NFL stadium naming rights agreements
- Evaluate the strategic fit between Amazon and the Commanders
- Identify potential Amazon technologies and services that could be activated in the stadium
- Develop additional founding partner and sponsorship opportunities

## Project Context
This project was developed as a hypothetical stadium naming rights valuation and sponsorship strategy exercise. It was designed to simulate the financial analysis, comparable-deal research, brand evaluation, and activation planning that could support an initial naming rights conversation.

The project was not commissioned by or affiliated with Amazon, the Washington Commanders, or any of the additional companies evaluated. All valuations are illustrative estimates based on the assumptions and comparable transactions used in the analysis.
